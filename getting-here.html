01 / Arriving at the conference

From the MRT to SMU

Use the sheltered basement concourse. Follow the overhead signs for the School of Accountancy or the Pacific Graphics 2026 signs along the path.

Choose your station below

Both routes continue past Koufu Food Court to the School of Accountancy (SOA). After your station directions, follow the shared route further down this page.

View the conference venue on Google Maps ↗
CC2Bras Basah Station Exit B · 2-minute walk⌄
  1. Leave Bras Basah Station through Exit B.
  2. Turn right and continue straight.
  3. Take the escalator up, then continue with the shared route to SOA.
DT21Bencoolen Station Exit C · 3-minute walk⌄
  1. Leave Bencoolen Station through Exit C.
  2. Turn left and continue straight.
  3. Walk past the SMU Gymnasium & Fitness Centre.
  4. Walk down the ramp and continue straight. FRANK by OCBC will be on your right.
  5. Take the escalator up, then continue with the shared route to SOA.

Both routes: continue to the School of Accountancy

  1. Pass Koufu Food Court. Follow the overhead signs towards the School of Accountancy / School of Law.
  2. Continue straight past the food court towards the end of the walkway.
  3. Before the lift lobby, turn right through the glass doors towards the School of Accountancy escalator.
  4. Look for the Ngee Ann Kongsi Auditorium arrow. Take the escalator up and turn right to reach the School of Accountancy.

02 / Welcome to PG2026

Entry & registration

Registration is on Level 2, by the Ngee Ann Kongsi Auditorium.

  1. At the Security Reception, enter through the visitor gantry on the left. Tell the security officer that you are attending the Pacific Graphics conference on Level 2.
  2. Use the escalator to Level 2. Follow the conference signs and turn right towards the Ngee Ann Kongsi Auditorium.
  3. Go to the registration counter for your last name: A–L or M–Z.
Bring identification. Keep your badge on.

Present your ID, passport, or student pass for registration verification. Wear your conference badge at all times during the conference.

04 / Tuesday, October 6 · 6:30 PM

Welcome dinner at Jyu Gae Bistro

51 Bras Basah Rd, #01-08 Lazada One, Singapore 189554

Wear your conference badge

Your badge is required for access and should be worn throughout the welcome dinner.

  1. Use the sheltered basement concourse. Follow the overhead signs for Lazada One or Exit E.
  2. Take the escalator up at Bras Basah MRT (CC2), Exit E.
  3. Turn left towards the Lazada One building.
  4. Follow the sheltered walkway around the exterior of Lazada One at ground level.
  5. Continue straight past the retail storefront glass windows for about 30–45 seconds. Look for the bright Jyu Gae Bistro signage and open patio seating at #01-08.
