About
Pacific Graphics

The 34th Pacific Conference on Computer Graphics and Applications (Pacific Graphics 2026) will be held in Singapore from October 20 to 23, 2026.

Pacific Graphics is the annual flagship conference of the Asia Graphics Association. As a highly-regarded international event, it provides a premium platform for researchers and practitioners to share the latest advances in computer graphics and interactive techniques.

Hosted by Singapore Management University (SMU), the conference will feature high-quality paper presentations, keynote speeches by world-renowned scholars, and various networking opportunities in the heart of Singapore.

PAPER TRACKS

Journal Papers

Accepted journal track papers will be published in a special issue of Computer Graphics Forum (CGF), the journal of the Eurographics Association.

Conference Papers

Accepted conference track papers will be published by the Eurographics Digital Library and presented at the conference.

Submission Process

  • All submissions must be original and not concurrently under review elsewhere.
  • Submit via SRM system: srmv2.eg.org
  • Reviewing process: Double-blind.
  • Submission length: 7–12 pages (excluding references).
